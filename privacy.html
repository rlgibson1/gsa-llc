GSA LLC — Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026. GSA LLC, 2237 Ketelsen Dr, Ames, IA 50010, USA.

GSA LLC sells automotive and truck accessories through online marketplaces, including our eBay store. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and what we do not do with it.

Marketplace orders

When you buy from us on a marketplace such as eBay, the marketplace provides us with the details needed to complete your order: your name, shipping address, the items ordered, and the order identifiers. We use that information only to fulfil your order, to answer your questions about it, and to handle returns or disputes through the marketplace.

  • We pass your name and shipping address to the distributor or carrier that ships your order, and to no one else.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information for marketing.
  • We do not build customer profiles or send you marketing messages.
  • Payments are processed by the marketplace. We never see or store your payment card details.

Our marketplace software

We use software of our own to run our marketplace accounts. It reads our own seller-account metrics, our own listings and offers, and our own orders in order to monitor account health, keep prices and stock levels current, and send orders to our distributors.

  • It keeps only aggregate counts, our own SKUs and offer identifiers, order identifiers, and dates.
  • It does not store buyer names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, or messages.
  • Order details needed to ship are used at the time of fulfilment and are not retained by our software afterwards.

Marketplace policies

Your relationship with the marketplace itself (account, payment, messaging, feedback) is governed by that marketplace's own privacy policy and user agreement, for example eBay's. Data you give the marketplace stays under those terms; this policy covers only what GSA LLC does with the details the marketplace passes to us.

Cookies and this website

This page is a static document. It sets no cookies and collects no analytics.

Your choices and contact

To ask what order information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted once your order and any return window are complete, contact us at sales@gsa-company.com. We answer within 10 business days.
